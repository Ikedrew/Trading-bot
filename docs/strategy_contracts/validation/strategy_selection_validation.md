## 2. MEAN_REVERSION (Simulated from Historical Data)

### Simulated conditions that would select MEAN_REVERSION:

Based on the 107 simulated selections from the impact analysis, the typical profile is:

| Evidence | Required | Typical Observed | Matches Hypothesis? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral macro | H4 neutral OR strength < 0.3 OR RANGING regime | Regime RANGING (86%), H4 null/NEUTRAL (73%) | YES — directionless environment |
| At range extreme | RP ≥ 0.70 or (RP ≤ 0.30 and > 0) | RP avg 0.78 (premium) or 0.18 (discount) | YES — overextended price |
| Structural level meaningful | H1 clarity ≥ 0.5 + swing/bos exists | Avg clarity 0.77, BOS present 100% | **PARTIALLY** — see below |

### Proxy Validation: "Structural level meaningful"

**Original concept:** "Institutional activity at this specific price level" (order block / FVG)
**Proxy used:** `(swing_high > 0 OR swing_low > 0 OR bos_level > 0) AND clarity >= 0.5`

| Does the proxy preserve the concept? | Assessment |
|---|---|
| "Institutions were active here" | **PARTIALLY.** Swing levels prove prior reactions occurred at structure. BOS level proves institutional commitment. But neither proves activity at the CURRENT extreme specifically. A swing_high at 1.09 doesn't mean there's institutional interest at 0.80 (current range_position). |
| False positive scenario | Price at premium extreme (RP=0.80) with H1 swing_high=1.09 (defined from a different price region). The swing EXISTS but may not be AT the current extreme. |
| Mitigating factor | The opportunity engine already validated "price at structural extreme" using range_position + BOS. If the strategy is reached, the opportunity-level location check already passed. |

### Potential False Positive Rate

**Estimated: LOW (~10-15%).** The combination of:
- RANGING regime (price IS oscillating)
- Range extreme (price IS stretched)
- Structural clarity ≥ 0.5 (structure IS clear)
- Swing levels exist (range boundaries ARE defined)

...is a REASONABLE proxy for "meaningful reversion opportunity." The ~10-15% false positive risk comes from cases where structure exists but is geographically distant from the current price extreme.

### NAS100 Concentration (43% of simulated selections)

NAS100 dominates because it spends significant time in RANGING regime with high structural clarity and frequent range extremes. This is NOT a false positive — indices DO mean-revert within established ranges. It's a correct strategy-market fit.

## 3. FALSE_BREAK (Simulated from Historical Data)

### Simulated conditions (16 selections):

| Evidence | Required | Typical Observed | Matches Hypothesis? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key level existed | Swing_high > 0 OR swing_low > 0 OR liquidity flags | Swing levels present (100%) | **PARTIALLY** — see below |
| Breakout failed (rejection) | M5 rejection present | Present (inferred from opportunity passing) | YES — failure confirmed |
| Level reclaimed | 0.2 < RP < 0.8 | Avg RP = 0.45 (solidly mid-range) | YES — price back inside |

### Proxy Validation: "Key level existed"

**Original concept:** "A breakout was ATTEMPTED at a level where stops clustered"
**Proxy used:** `swing_high > 0 OR swing_low > 0` (swing boundaries exist)

| Does the proxy preserve the concept? | Assessment |
|---|---|
| "Level existed that could be broken" | **YES** — swing levels ARE the boundaries breakout traders target |
| "Breakout was actually attempted" | **NO** — the proxy doesn't prove price REACHED the level. It only proves the level EXISTS. |
| False positive scenario | Price is at RP=0.45 (mid-range) with a swing_high far above. Price never reached the swing_high — there was no breakout. But the conditions fire because: level exists + rejection happened + price is mid-range. |
| Mitigating factor | The COMBINATION of rejection + mid-range IMPLIES something happened at the extreme. If rejection is present and price is mid-range, price was likely recently at an extreme (rejected) and came back. |

### Potential False Positive Rate

**Estimated: MODERATE (20-30%).** The proxy chain (swing exists + rejection + mid-range) can fire in situations where:
- Rejection was at a mid-range level (not at the boundary)
- Price was never at the extreme (just choppy mid-range with a wick)

The FALSE_BREAK hypothesis specifically requires "was at extreme, failed, came back" — a temporal sequence. The single-snapshot proxy approximates but doesn't guarantee this sequence.

### Risk Assessment

16 selections out of 1,305 evaluations (1.2%) — even with 20-30% false positive rate, that's ~3-5 false positives total. Very low absolute volume. Downstream entry/risk gates provide additional filtering.

| Mid-range chop misclassified | Choppy price action with random wick produces rejection + mid-range | ~10% | LOW — confidence score is low for these |

### TREND_CONTINUATION

No false positives — all conditions use direct, reliable signals.

## 6. Conclusion

The proxy substitution **preserves the core market concepts** for all three strategies:
- TREND_CONTINUATION: no proxy needed, direct signals
- MEAN_REVERSION: proxy correctly identifies "ranging + extreme + structure present" (the hypothesis)
- FALSE_BREAK: proxy correctly identifies "level + rejection + reclaim" (the temporal approximation)

The main weakness is **temporal**: both MEAN_REVERSION and FALSE_BREAK ideally need "what happened before this moment" — which a single-snapshot model can only approximate. The proxies are the BEST AVAILABLE given this architectural constraint.

**Recommendation for future validation:** After live production with reconciled code, compare strategy selections against actual trade outcomes (R-multiple). If MEAN_REVERSION selections produce worse outcomes than TREND_CONTINUATION, the proxy may need tightening. If they produce comparable or better outcomes, the proxy is validated empirically.
