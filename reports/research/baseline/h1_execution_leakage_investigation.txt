STEP 4: SHADOW MODEL vs LIVE EXECUTION — STRUCTURAL DIFFERENCES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  SHADOW MODEL:
    Entry: (bid+ask)/2 midpoint at decision time
    SL: V10 RiskEngine computed stop (from OrderIntent.sl)
    TP: V10 EntryEngine computed target (from OrderIntent.tp)
    Exit: First of SL/TP/60-bar timeout
    SL checked BEFORE TP on same bar
    No spread deduction
    No commission
    No slippage
    No trade management (no trailing, no BE, no partial)
    Position held until mechanical exit only

  LIVE EXECUTION:
    Entry: Actual broker fill (includes slippage)
    SL: May be modified by TradeStateManager (BE, trailing)
    TP: May be modified
    Exit: Broker-confirmed close (SL/TP/management/manual)
    Spread paid on entry AND exit
    Commission charged
    Slippage on entry and exit
    Trade management active (trailing stop, breakeven, partial exits)

A. PROVEN:
   - V10_PRIMARY and Live trades are DIFFERENT POPULATIONS
   - V10_PRIMARY = 985 shadows (all EXECUTE decisions)
   - Live trades = 94 (broker fills only)
   - Entity overlap: 35
   - The +0.76R 'gap' is NOT a paired leakage measurement
   - It compares two different population means (unpaired)

B. PLAUSIBLE EXPLANATIONS for the gap:
   1. POPULATION BIAS: V10_PRIMARY includes 858 shadows for opportunities
      that were guard-blocked or broker-rejected. These may have BETTER
      geometry than the 94 that actually filled (selection effect).
   2. SHADOW MODEL OPTIMISM: No spread/commission/slippage in shadow.
      For each trade at ~2 pips spread cost ≈ 0.1-0.3R per trade.
   3. TRADE MANAGEMENT: Live uses trailing/BE which may cut winners short
      and not be captured by the shadow's simple SL/TP/timeout model.
   4. TEMPORAL MISMATCH: Primary shadows and live trades may not cover
      the same time period (shadow data accumulates continuously,
      live trades only from the period V10 was execution-enabled).

C. SHADOW MODEL LIMITATIONS:
   - Entry at midpoint (live pays spread at entry)
   - No spread deduction (≈0.1-0.3R per trade)
   - No commission
   - SL checked before TP same bar (pessimistic for shadow)
   - 60-bar timeout (live has no forced timeout)
   - No trade management (live has trailing/BE)

D. UNRESOLVED:
   - Whether the 35 overlapping entities show actual leakage
   - Whether trade management systematically helps or hurts
   - What proportion of the 858 non-filled shadows would have been profitable
   - Whether guard-blocking is removing good opportunities

The original H1 framing ('execution leakage') is MISLEADING.

The +0.76R gap is NOT primarily execution leakage.
It is a POPULATION DIFFERENCE between:
  - ALL V10 EXECUTE decisions (952 opportunities, many never filled)
  - ACTUALLY FILLED trades (94 that survived guards + broker)

The correct decomposition is:
  Gap = Population_bias + Execution_costs + Management_effects + Model_limitations
