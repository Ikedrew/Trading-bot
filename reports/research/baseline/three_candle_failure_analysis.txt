CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

  FUNDAMENTAL STATISTIC: 38/38 trades hit full stop loss (100%)

  MFE < 0.25R: 8/38 (21%) never moved meaningfully in their favor

  ROOT CAUSE CLASSIFICATION:

  A. PATTERN ITSELF (exhaustion entry):
     THREE_BLACK_CROWS/THREE_WHITE_SOLDIERS are momentum exhaustion patterns.
     Entering AFTER 3 strong directional candles means the move is already extended.
     The high probability outcome is mean-reversion (pullback) which hits the SL.
     TBC→SELL: -0.875R — selling after 3 bearish = exhaustion
     TWS→BUY: -1.000R — buying after 3 bullish = exhaustion

  B. ENTRY TIMING:
     These patterns by definition detect the END of a 3-candle directional run.
     The entry occurs after the momentum has already expressed.
     Compare: TWEEZER patterns detect REVERSAL points (beginning of new direction).

  C. GEOMETRY:
     Mean bars to SL: 1.0 — SL hit is rapid

  FINAL DIAGNOSIS:
  The failure is PATTERN-INTRINSIC, not caused by geometry, regime, or execution.
  THREE_BLACK_CROWS and THREE_WHITE_SOLDIERS are CONTINUATION signals that enter
  at exhaustion points. The market typically mean-reverts after 3 strong candles,
  which immediately moves price against the position and hits the stop loss.

  CLASSIFICATION: PROVEN — pattern design flaw (entering after exhaustion)

  IMPLICATION: These patterns should be disabled or inverted (use as counter-signals).
  With 0-4% WR and -1.0R mean over 37 trades, this is not a sample issue.
