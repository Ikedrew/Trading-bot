  Mean-reversion SHOULD degrade with displacement because:
  1. The structural zone was identified as support (demand)
  2. When price moves far above, the zone may have been 'consumed' (filled)
  3. Higher displacement = price has trended = mean-reversion thesis weakens
  4. Geometry compression (less reward remaining) mechanically limits upside
  5. The broker physically cannot fill stale geometry (TP < ask)
