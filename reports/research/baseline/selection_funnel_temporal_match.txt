FINAL CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

FINDING 1: THE 985→94 GAP IS NOT PRIMARILY GUARD-CAUSED — PROVEN
  Decision ledger shows only 363 V10 EXECUTE intentions during its period.
  Of 363: 216 blocked by guards, 54 broker-rejected, 94 filled = ~364 (±1)
  The remaining 623 shadows come from a period BEFORE the decision ledger existed
  or from a mode where decisions were not logged (shadow-only operation).

FINDING 2: GUARD BLOCK RATE = 59.5% — PROVEN
  Of opportunities that REACH the guard chain: 216/363 = 59.5% blocked.
  Top blockers: correlation_guard (47%), portfolio_exposure (37%), daily_limit (11%)


FINDING 4: BROKER REJECTION = 54 TRADES — PROVEN
  54 opportunities passed ALL guards but broker rejected.
  Reason: 'execution_failed:broker_rejected' (100%)
  These represent spread/price-change rejections at order submission time.

FINDING 5: 623 'PRE-LEDGER' SHADOWS — DATA LIMITATION
  These shadows were created during a period without decision_ledger logging,
  OR during shadow-only mode where V10 ran without execution enabled.
  They cannot be attributed to guard filtering vs other causes.

HIGHEST-CONFIDENCE EXPLANATION:
  Only 94 of 985 became live because:
  1. ~623 shadows come from non-execution periods (shadow-only observation)
  2. Of the ~363 during execution, 59.5% were guard-blocked (216)
  3. Of the remaining ~147, 37% were broker-rejected (54)
  4. The surviving 94 (64% of guard-passed) actually filled

  The guards are NOT score-selective (blocked mean score 5.2 vs passed 5.4)
  but are POSITION-STATE selective (correlation, exposure, daily limit).
  Whether this position-state selection is quality-improving or quality-destroying
  depends on the shadow R comparison above.

NEXT EXPERIMENT:

  by checking shadow creation dates. If confirmed, the real funnel is 363→94 (74% loss),
  not 985→94 (90% loss). The dominant filtering is guards (60%) + broker (15%).
