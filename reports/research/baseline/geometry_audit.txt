QUESTION: Is 'SL_ABOVE_ENTRY' a REAL BUG or a REFERENCE-PRICE MISINTERPRETATION?

  ANSWER: REFERENCE-PRICE MISINTERPRETATION
  PROOF: 32 ACCEPTED BUY orders ALSO have SL > entry_reference
  Therefore SL > entry_reference is NOT the condition that causes rejection.

  The V10 entry_reference is a STRUCTURAL ESTIMATE (midpoint/BOS level)
  that can be BELOW the actual ask (execution price).
  When entry_ref < SL < ask, the geometry is VALID for the broker
  because the broker evaluates SL against ask, not against entry_reference.

  The ACTUAL rejection cause for the 30 'Invalid stops' orders must be:
  1. SL too close to current price (broker stops_level minimum)
  2. Price moved between decision and order submission
  3. TP already passed (price already at/beyond target)
  4. Broker-specific minimum stop distance constraint
